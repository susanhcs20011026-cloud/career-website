01 / EDUCATIONAL EMAIL

The Hidden Profit Killer

Audience
Freelancers, independent professionals, and small business owners
Content Approach
Education-first storytelling
My Role
Original Copywriter
ANNOTATED VIEW

Archived writing sample. Numerical claims in this draft have not been independently verified and are not presented as current research findings.

SUBJECT LINE

The real reason projects go over budget (it's not what you think)

→Curiosity-driven subject line

The subject line challenges a familiar assumption about project budgets, creating curiosity without introducing the product too early.

PREHEADER

52% of projects experience this problem. Here's what the data shows.

Your last project probably cost more than you planned.

→Problem-first storytelling

Opens with a recognizable financial frustration instead of a product pitch.

Not because you estimated wrong. Not because the client was difficult. But because of something quieter that happens in small increments: scope creep. Here's what we learned from analyzing thousands of projects:

52% of all projects experience scope creep:

That's more than half. And when it happens, 85% of those projects exceed their original budget by an average of 27%.

Think about your last project. Did you:

  • Add "just one more revision" that turned into three?
  • Include a feature that wasn't in the original agreement?
  • Spend extra hours on client requests without adjusting your rate?
→Audience recognition

Turns an abstract business concept into an everyday client interaction freelancers can recognize.

That's scope creep. The problem isn't the individual requests (each one seems small). The problem is what happens when they accumulate.

The math gets ugly fast:

Start with a $5,000 project. Add 27% from scope creep. You're now doing $6,350 worth of work for $5,000. That's $1,350 in unbilled hours. Work on 10 projects like this per year? You've just given away $13,500.

→Concrete financial example

Translates scope creep into a tangible financial consequence, making the cost easier to visualize.

The worst part: scope creep can multiply costs up to 4 times the initial project budget in extreme cases.

Why this keeps happening:

Most clients don't intentionally create scope creep. They're not trying to take advantage. They're genuinely excited about the project and keep thinking of improvements.

→Empathetic framing

Avoids blaming clients and frames the issue as a common business-management challenge.

But without clear boundaries, "Can we add this one thing?" becomes the most expensive question in solopreneurship.

Need better tools to track your projects?

→Product transition

Moves from education to product relevance after establishing why the problem matters.

Indy helps solopreneurs manage projects, contracts, and invoices in one place.

→Benefit-focused product messaging

Connects the reader's workflow challenge to relevant product capabilities without introducing an unrelated feature list.

ARCHIVED PROMOTIONAL COPY · HISTORICAL OFFER

02 / B2B EMAIL COPYWRITING

B2B Partnership Outreach

Audience
Potential strategic business partners
My Role
Original Copywriter
Focus
Audience alignment, mutual value proposition, and concise professional communication

Edited, anonymized portfolio adaptation

Subject: Exploring a Strategic Partnership Opportunity

Hi [Partnership Team],

I'm reaching out from Indy, an all-in-one business management platform that helps freelancers and independent professionals manage proposals, contracts, invoicing, time tracking, and client projects.

We understand that your platform helps independent professionals discover opportunities, connect with clients, and grow their freelance careers. This makes our audiences highly complementary: while your platform supports freelancers in finding new work, Indy helps them manage their business operations after securing a project.

→Audience Research

Demonstrates an understanding of the potential partner's business model and target audience before introducing the collaboration proposal.

→Complementary Value Proposition

Connects the two platforms through different stages of the freelancer journey, establishing a clear reason for collaboration.

We see an opportunity to collaborate in ways that create value for both platforms and our shared freelancer community.

Specifically, we'd love to explore:

  • Freelancer Referrals: Introduce your platform to Indy's freelancer community through relevant educational content, onboarding communications, and email outreach.
  • Exclusive Member Benefits: Explore a co-branded offer or bundled promotion that gives freelancers additional value when using both platforms.
  • Cross-Promotion Opportunities: Introduce Indy Pro to your community through relevant marketing channels, helping freelancers manage their projects, contracts, and invoicing more efficiently.
→Actionable Partnership Ideas

Transforms a general partnership request into three concrete opportunities: referrals, exclusive member benefits, and cross-promotion.

We believe these opportunities could help both platforms reach relevant audiences while supporting freelancers throughout their professional journey.

Would you be open to a brief 15-minute conversation to explore a potential collaboration?

→Low-Friction CTA

Ends with a specific, low-commitment next step that makes it easy for the recipient to respond.

Best,
Indy Team
